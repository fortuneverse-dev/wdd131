CareLink Virtual Clinic

Website plan

Site Name

CareLink Virtual Clinic

This name tells visitors two things right away. “CareLink” shows that the site connects patients with care, and “Virtual Clinic” shows that the consultations happen online. The logo combines a video camera with a medical cross to show the same idea.

Optional domain availability: carelink-virtual.org

Site Purpose

The site helps patients understand virtual consultations and plan one from home. It explains how an online visit works, lists the types of consultations offered, and gives patients a form to request an appointment.

Planned pages

  • Home: what a virtual consultation is, how it works in three steps, and its benefits.
  • Services: the types of consultations, such as general medicine, nutrition, and follow-up visits.
  • Book a Consultation: a form to choose a service, a date, and a time. The appointment is saved in the browser.
  • References: sources for all images and text, linked in the footer.

Scenarios

These are questions a visitor from the target audience might ask. The content of the site will answer each one.

  • How does a virtual consultation work, and what do I need to take part?
  • Which types of consultations can I have online?
  • How can I book a consultation for a date and time that suits me?
  • Can I see or cancel the appointment I booked?

Color Schema

The colors below are used on this plan and will be used on the final website.

Selected colors and where they will be used
Color Hex code Where it is used
Deep green #2A5F4F Header, footer, headings, links, and table headers
Mist #D9E2E0 Page background
Leaf green #98B839 Accent: underline under each section heading
Sage green #5DA78E Accent: side border on the font samples
Light lime #C0DC81 Buttons and the logo circle
Cream green #E1EBC1 Alternate table rows
Dark ink #1F2E27 Body text and paragraphs

The green palette suggests health, tranquility, and nature, which fits a healthcare site. White text on deep green and dark ink on the light greens keep the text easy to read.

Typography

Source Serif 4: headings

See a doctor from home

Used for the site name, page titles, and section headings. Its serif shapes feel calm and trustworthy.

Nunito Sans: body text

Choose a service, pick a time, and meet your provider online.

Used for paragraphs, navigation, form labels, and buttons. It is clear and easy to read on small screens.
